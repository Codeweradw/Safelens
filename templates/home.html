Home Safety

Keep Doors and Windows Locked

This prevents intruders and keeps your home safe even when you are inside. It reduces the chances of someone entering without your knowledge.

Install Proper Lighting Outside

Well-lit areas discourage suspicious people from coming near. It helps you see movements around the house more clearly.

Do Not Share Your Daily Routine Publicly

Avoid posting your schedule on social media to reduce risks of targeted break-ins. Keeping details offline protects your security.
